One free business management system for daily operations

Most small businesses start with a notebook, a spreadsheet and a separate till. The result is duplicated work: stock counts that do not match sales, invoices retyped from memory, and month-end reports assembled by hand. A free business management system removes that duplication by letting a single transaction update every affected record.

OpsPro is that system. It is a Windows desktop application from AlpherTech that combines a point of sale with inventory, purchasing, receivables, bookkeeping, employee records, project tracking and reporting. Because all modules read and write the same local database, the numbers in a report always match the sales and stock movements behind them.

Inventory management software that keeps stock honest

OpsPro records stock at product and warehouse level. Each sale reduces the quantity, each received purchase order increases it, and transfers move units between locations. Products carry a minimum and maximum level, so the dashboard can flag items that need reordering before they run out. Expiry dates add a second alert for goods with a shelf life, which matters for agro-input shops and pharmacies.

Voiding a sale restores the stock it consumed and marks the transaction as void rather than deleting it, so stock counts and audit history stay aligned. Inventory valuation reports use average cost to show what is tied up in stock and the expected margin on what is on the shelf.

Accounting and financial reporting for small teams

The accounting module is built for owners and bookkeepers rather than full-time accountants. A chart of accounts organizes assets, liabilities, equity, revenue and expenses. Sales and expenses post automatically, and manual journal entries use a debit and credit account with generated entry numbers. Bank reconciliation matches statement lines to recorded transactions, and petty cash tracks small in-and-out movements.

At period end, the trial balance, profit and loss statement and balance sheet can be reviewed on screen or exported. Combined with POS receipt printing and Z-reports, this gives a small business the same control loop larger retailers take for granted.

Frequently asked questions

What is a free business management system?

A free business management system is software that runs core business operations without a subscription fee. OpsPro combines point of sale, inventory, purchasing, customers, suppliers, accounting, human resources, projects and reporting in a single Windows application.

What business documents can OpsPro generate?

OpsPro generates invoices, thermal receipts, purchase orders, journal entries, Z-reports, trial balances, profit and loss statements, balance sheets and weekly report PDFs. Report data can also be exported to Excel.

Does OpsPro include inventory management?

Yes. OpsPro tracks stock per product and per warehouse, supports purchase receiving and stock transfers, flags low stock and expiring products, and updates quantities automatically when sales are completed or voided.

Can OpsPro handle accounting and financial statements?

OpsPro includes a chart of accounts, manual and automatic journal entries, bank reconciliation, petty cash tracking, trial balance, profit and loss and balance sheet reports.

Is OpsPro suitable for multi-user teams?

Yes. OpsPro supports Admin, Manager and Cashier roles with different permissions. Every create, update and delete action is written to an audit log so changes can be traced to a user and timestamp.

Where is OpsPro business data stored?

All data is stored in a local SQLite database on the Windows device. Automatic and manual backups can be configured, and a restore replaces the current database with a backup file.
