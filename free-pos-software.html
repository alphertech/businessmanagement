What is free POS software?

Point of sale software is the program a shop uses to record a sale, calculate the total, take payment and issue a receipt. A free POS software gives you those checkout capabilities without a monthly licence, which matters for small retailers, pharmacies, agro-input dealers and restaurants that operate on thin margins.

OpsPro takes that idea further: it is a complete business management system, not just a till. Every sale recorded at the counter updates stock levels, customer balances, accounting entries and reports at the same time. The software is built by AlpherTech for East African businesses and ships as a Windows desktop application with local data storage.

Why businesses choose OpsPro as their free POS system

Fast checkout

Search by name, SKU or barcode, tap a product card, adjust quantity and complete the sale with the F12 shortcut.

Barcode scanning

USB scanners that send an Enter keystroke work out of the box. Rapid keystrokes are detected as a scan.

Receipt printing

Offline by design

Receipt printing on ESC/POS thermal printers

Receipt printing is one of the reasons shops move from handwritten books to a POS. OpsPro prints through ESC/POS thermal printers on the same local network. Set the printer address once in Settings, and every completed sale can print an itemised receipt showing the invoice number, products, quantities, unit prices, tax, total, amount paid and outstanding balance.

If a customer needs a second copy, open the sale in Sales Management and reprint it. Because receipts are generated from stored transaction data rather than retyped, the copy always matches the original. For shops that also issue invoices and statements, see the business document generator guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a free POS software for Windows?

Yes. OpsPro is a free POS software for Windows 10 and Windows 11. You download the installer, create a local security PIN or password, and start selling without a monthly subscription or cloud account.

Can a free POS system print receipts?

OpsPro prints itemised receipts on ESC/POS thermal printers connected over your local network. You can configure the printer IP in Settings, print after completing a sale, and reprint any receipt from the sales history.

Does a free POS system work offline?

OpsPro works fully offline. Products, sales, customers and receipts are stored in a local SQLite database on the Windows computer, so checkout continues when the internet or power is unstable.

What payment methods can I record in OpsPro?

OpsPro records Cash, Credit, Mobile Money and Bank Transfer payments. Credit sales are checked against the customer's credit limit, and the Z-report shows a breakdown of sales by payment method.

Do I need a barcode scanner to use OpsPro?

A barcode scanner is optional. OpsPro supports USB barcode scanners that send an Enter keystroke, and you can also search products by name or SKU and tap product cards to add them to the cart.

How much does OpsPro POS software cost?

OpsPro is free to download and use. There is no subscription fee, no per-terminal charge and no cloud storage bill. AlpherTech supports the product through email and phone channels in Uganda.
