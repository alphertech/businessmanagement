Who AlpherTech is

AlpherTech Solutions (often written AlpherTech) is a software team based in Kampala, Uganda. It builds practical desktop tools for businesses that need dependable records without cloud subscriptions. Its flagship product, OpsPro, combines a free POS system with business management software for inventory, purchasing, accounting, HR, projects and reporting.

The team's approach is deliberately local-first. Business data stays in a database on the customer's own computer, backups are under the owner's control, and the software keeps working when the internet does not. That design choice comes from working with East African retailers who deal with unreliable connectivity, power interruptions and tight operating budgets.

Technology and data ownership

OpsPro is a Flutter desktop application for Windows backed by SQLite, with ESC/POS thermal printing, PDF generation and Excel export. Data remains on the device, and every create, update and delete action is written to an audit log. Backup and restore are built in, so a business can keep its own copies of its records.

The same engineering choices support the document generator: invoices, purchase orders and financial statements are produced from the stored records rather than from separate templates, which keeps paperwork aligned with the books.
